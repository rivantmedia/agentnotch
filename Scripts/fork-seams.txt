#   SEAM   <id>   <file>   <text that must appear in the file, verbatim>   [<n>]
#                          with <n>: on at least n lines (one per call path)

#                          that isn't a comment (#, //, @# after indentation)>
#   ALLOW  <path or glob>          a path under Sources/, Tests/ or windows/ that
#                                  may differ from upstream (everything else may not)
#
# Globs: a trailing /** matches everything below a folder.
